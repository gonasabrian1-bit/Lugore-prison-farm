✅ OBJECTIVES OF THE FARM


✔️ To use farming or production as rehabilitation programs of offenders

📖 BACKGROUND OF THE FARM


The establishment of UGPFL was in 1950 and occupies 2418 acres of land. UGPFL is located in Lugore Parish, Owalo Sub-county, Aswa County and Gulu District.


It is 33Km north of Gulu city along road heading to Lamwo District. On these 2418 acres of land, there are various activities that take place and these include crop production, livestock management and afforestation.


Under afforestation, the clonal hybrid eucalyptus on 120 acres, under crop production, the major crops grown are maize and sun flower on 1300 acres, under orchard establishment 75 acres are occupied where by 55 acres are occupied by grafted mangoes and 20 acres are occupied by budded oranges. More so, are short fruit trees like jack fruit, avocado, bananas among others under establishment and well cover ten additional acres.


The Farm also undertakes vegetable growing covering ten acres. This is made for the inmate nutritional needs and demand.


The following are the vegetables planted; onions, eggplants, okra, tomatoes, sukuma wiki and cabbage etc.
